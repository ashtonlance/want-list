How prices work

A price marked "shipped" is the best deal found with shipping included. Other prices are the TCGplayer market price (recent sales, before shipping) on the date shown, with the cheapest current listing beside it.

"Check price" queues a card for Claude to find the cheapest seller including shipping. The DoubleHolo link opens the card's set on DoubleHolo, or the card itself once you save its DoubleHolo link. Card images and prices from TCGplayer.
